# Audio credits

Third-party audio in the game and its licence. Sounds built from the Sonniss GDC bundle (tools/audio/*_recorded.py)
follow that bundle's own licence; the rules for using it are in docs/plan/sound-lane.md.

## Music

- `audio/music/match_theme.ogg`: "Epic boss battle" by Juhani Junkala (OpenGameArt.org,
  https://opengameart.org/content/boss-battle-music), licensed CC0 1.0 Universal
  (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Found through Patch's asset search, which converted it from WAV
  to Ogg Vorbis and put the credit in the Ogg comments. Copied into the game unchanged. CC0 asks for no credit; it is
  recorded here anyway. Plays during a match (scripts/audio/map_audio.gd).
- `audio/music/main_theme.ogg`: made with Suno for this game (Preston's take 1). Menu theme, not hooked up yet.
