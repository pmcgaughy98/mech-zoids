# Gorilla shield-tech recreation

Preston expanded the redesign scope: "next, also these are so good i think were going to redo all. do gorilla next". Gorilla moves ahead of pangolin. Existing bodies and kits stay available while new designs are reviewed.

Keep its green hexagonal shield technology and protector identity. Strengthen the physical power system: huge built-in forearm hex projectors, a visible sternum core, thick shoulder conduits and a dorsal hex shield generator with armoured side vanes. All equipment is mounted to a massive four-contact knuckle-standing gorilla body. Small low head, enormous shoulders/forearms, compact hindquarters, no tail. Emerald body, lime lenses, copper-orange projector bevels, charcoal frame and steel contact pads.

No weapon or shield is held. The forearm generators are attached parts; active shield shells and domes are separate effects. This is a body proposal, not a change to the existing chosen shield kit or balance. One new built-in image using only the Drembit craft reference, then four separate white modeling inputs at a shared scale. No old gorilla art supplied. No MeshForge run or automatic image retry. Actual built-in image token/cost metering is unavailable.
