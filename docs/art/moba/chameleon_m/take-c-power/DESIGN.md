# Chameleon optical-power recreation

Preston requested "Chameleon next" after the rhino's leg revision. Theme: refraction and optical cloaking. Preserve the chosen invisibility/scouting identity while making its power source physically visible at rest.

Proposed body: turquoise-teal mechanical chameleon, large cyan prism-lens turret eyes with gold bezels, three broad refracting flank plates per side, four violet cloak emitter nodes at the limb roots, and a low dorsal refraction cassette. Thick armoured articulated limbs with robust clamp feet avoid the rhino's thin-pylon problem. Tail is long, straight and segmented for consistent modeling/animation. Active camouflage/decoys and beams stay separate from this neutral reference.

One new built-in image with only Drembit as craft reference. No older chameleon art supplied. Four modeling views packaged on separate white sheets at a common scale and contact baseline. Existing takes/kit/game files unchanged; no MeshForge run or automatic image retry. Exact body and any additional kit change remain proposals for visual review. Built-in image token/cost metering is not available.
