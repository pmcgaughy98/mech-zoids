# Cobra jungler concept

Preston selected Cobra as a jungler and suggested a passive for slithering across/over walls. Animal and role selected; traversal mechanic tentative. Proposed body: emerald/charcoal legless cobra, gold clamps, amber optics, hood phase projectors, front neck core and cyan ventral adhesion/phase pads along articulated segments.

Passive proposal: approaching a traversable wall while moving can trigger a brief wall-crossing slither, consuming a cooldown and leaving a visible trail. Crossing ends at valid walkable ground; no parking inside terrain. Limit which terrain and traversal distance after playtesting. No final name, values, immunity or kit mechanics chosen.
