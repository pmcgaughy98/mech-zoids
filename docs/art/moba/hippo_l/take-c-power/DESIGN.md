# Hippo power redesign

Preston approved the recreation proposal and order: hippo, kangaroo, pangolin, shoebill, falcon, lobster, one at a time. This is the first design review. Existing hippo takes and game assets stay intact.

Approved direction: delirium and restorative energy, a luminous violet belly chamber, liquid-filled side reservoirs, and jaw vents for violet mist. The machine must reveal its power source while standing. The hippo shape carries that equipment: huge blunt muzzle, low barrel torso, short stout legs, broad planted feet. Its chambers and channels illuminate as it gathers or releases energy. External effects remain separate from modeling inputs.

This is a visual design proposal, not a final ability rework. Existing healing, weakening and area-control mechanics can inform later kit work. Names, lore and balance remain Preston's. No changes to kit code or to existing art. No other god's appearance or symbols used.
