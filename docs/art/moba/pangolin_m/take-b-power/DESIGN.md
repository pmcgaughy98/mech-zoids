# Pangolin magnetic-power recreation

Preston requested "next" after the shoebill; pangolin is next in the approved order. Theme: magnetism and repulsion, a physical field-generating plate system supporting the armoured rolling identity. This is a new body proposal, not a change to the chosen rolling mechanics or balance.

Copper-bronze overlapping plates on visible articulated magnetic mounts; paired cobalt/ice-blue spine rails; one recessed U-shaped field coupler per flank; a pelvis charge window and matching tail recesses. Plates stay physically attached in the modeling pose. Four substantial armoured limbs, integrated steel claw feet and a long straight segmented tail. No floating armour, force effects, spikes, thin pylon legs or curled tail.

One built-in image with only Drembit as craft reference; no previous pangolin art supplied. Four modeling views and four separate white sheets at one common scale. Save raw unchanged; preserve existing takes, kit and game assets. No automatic retry or MeshForge run. Actual generation usage/cost not exposed. Final body selection remains Preston's.
