Pixel-art animation sheet of ONE mech rhino doing a horn toss: it scoops low and flicks its horn up and back over its body, on a plain magenta background (#FF00FF). The three attached sprites are the exact design: the first is the front view, the second the side view facing right, the third the back view. Keep everything from them: big flat hexagonal yellow armour plates with the dark frame showing between them, short stubby legs with big round grey disc wheel joints, flat grey feet, two-tone grey nose horn and a small second horn, red eye, grey jaw, short tail stub, the same colours, outline and chunky pixel size. Only the pose changes. Never redesign: no gloss, no rounded armour, no long or realistic legs, no new parts.

Layout: a grid of 3 rows and 7 columns of equal cells, one whole rhino per cell, nothing crossing a cell edge, no labels, no text, no grid lines. Row 1 is the front view: camera directly in front, centred and left-right symmetric, like the first sprite. Row 2 is the side view facing right, like the second sprite. Row 3 is the back view: camera directly behind, centred and symmetric, tail stub toward the viewer, no face, like the third sprite. Each column is one moment seen from three sides, so the three rows match frame for frame. The rhino is the same size in every cell and the ground line is at the same height in every cell. Its legs keep their length in every view: in the back view, when the front end rises, the shoulders and head rise above the hips while the hind legs stay as they are.

Columns, left to right:
1. Reference: exactly the attached sprites, standing still with all four feet flat on the ground.
2. Crouch: front legs bent, head dropped low to the ground, horn pointing forward at ground level.
3. Scoop: a short step forward, head still low, horn sliding forward along the ground.
4. Flick start: head swings up hard, the front end starts to rise.
5. Flick peak: head thrown up and back, horn pointing up and backward over the body, front feet slightly off the ground, back feet planted.
6. Settle: front feet back on the ground, head coming down.
7. Recover: back to the reference stance.
The back feet stay planted in every column. No enemy is drawn.

No motion lines, no dust, no impact effect, no shadow, no other creature.
