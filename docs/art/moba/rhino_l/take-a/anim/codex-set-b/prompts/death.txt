Pixel-art animation sheet of ONE mech rhino being destroyed and collapsing, on a plain magenta background (#FF00FF). The three attached sprites are the exact design: the first is the front view, the second the side view facing right, the third the back view. Keep everything from them: big flat hexagonal yellow armour plates with the dark frame showing between them, short stubby legs with big round grey disc wheel joints, flat grey feet, two-tone grey nose horn and a small second horn, red eye, grey jaw, short tail stub, the same colours, outline and chunky pixel size. Only the pose changes. Never redesign: no gloss, no rounded armour, no long or realistic legs, no new parts.

Layout: a grid of 3 rows and 7 columns of equal cells, one whole rhino per cell, nothing crossing a cell edge, no labels, no text, no grid lines. Row 1 is the front view: camera directly in front, centred and left-right symmetric, like the first sprite. Row 2 is the side view facing right, like the second sprite. Row 3 is the back view: camera directly behind, centred and symmetric, tail stub toward the viewer, no face, like the third sprite. Each column is one moment seen from three sides, so the three rows match frame for frame. The rhino is the same size in every cell and the ground line is at the same height in every cell. Its legs keep their length in every view: in the back view, when the front end rises, the shoulders and head rise above the hips while the hind legs stay as they are.

Columns, left to right:
1. Reference: exactly the attached sprites, standing still with all four feet flat on the ground.
2. Hit: the body jolts, head thrown back slightly.
3. Stagger: front legs buckle, body tilting forward.
4. Collapse: chest hits the ground, back legs still standing.
5. Fall: back end drops, the body lies flat on its belly with the legs splayed out to the sides.
6. Down: head drops to the ground, the eye light goes out (the eye turns dark grey instead of red).
7. Still: the same as column 6, settled one pixel lower.
In every view it falls onto its belly. No parts fly off, no fire, no smoke, no sparks.

No motion lines, no dust, no impact effect, no shadow, no other creature.
