Pixel-art animation sheet of ONE mech rhino shuffling forward while braced: it stays locked low in its braced stance and creeps forward with small heavy steps, on a plain magenta background (#FF00FF). The three attached sprites are the exact design and the exact braced pose: the first is the front view, the second the side view facing right, the third the back view. Keep everything from them: big flat hexagonal yellow armour plates with the dark frame showing between them, short stubby legs with big round grey disc wheel joints, flat grey feet, two-tone grey nose horn and a small second horn, red eye, grey jaw, short tail stub, the same colours, outline and chunky pixel size. Only the legs change. Never redesign: no gloss, no rounded armour, no long or realistic legs, no new parts.

Layout: a grid of 3 rows and 6 columns of equal cells, one whole rhino per cell, nothing crossing a cell edge, no labels, no text, no grid lines. Row 1 is the front view: camera directly in front, centred and left-right symmetric, like the first sprite. Row 2 is the side view facing right, like the second sprite. Row 3 is the back view: camera directly behind, centred and symmetric, tail stub toward the viewer, no face, like the third sprite. Each column is one moment seen from three sides, so the three rows match frame for frame. The rhino is the same size in every cell and the ground line is at the same height in every cell. Its legs keep their length in every view.

Columns, left to right:
1. Reference: exactly the attached braced sprites, all four feet planted wide.
2. The rhino's own left front foot lifts just clear of the ground, two or three pixels, and reaches a short way forward; the other three feet stay planted.
3. That foot plants again; the right hind foot lifts just clear and slides forward.
4. The right front foot lifts just clear and reaches a short way forward; the other three stay planted.
5. That foot plants again; the left hind foot lifts just clear and slides forward.

No motion lines, no dust, no impact effect, no shadow, no other creature.
