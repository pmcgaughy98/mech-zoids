# Rhino seismic-power recreation

Preston requested "rhino next" after the gorilla. His earlier direction was some relation to tremors/earth, with details undecided. This body proposes a coherent seismic system without treating it as a final kit choice: squat dorsal drive, shoulder transducers, compression-piston feet, resonant snout horn and an illuminated chest window.

Golden-yellow/ochre armour, slate-blue/charcoal chassis, cool steel contact parts and mint-teal resonance channels. Physical machined plates and piston feet communicate ground force while idle. Active tremors, cracks and debris remain separate effects. Preserve the animal's central snout horn, small secondary horn, barrel torso, four pillar legs and short tail.

One built-in image with only Drembit as craft reference. No old rhino art supplied, no image retry, no MeshForge run or changes to existing kit/game assets. Four modeling views are packaged onto separate 1024-square white sheets at a shared scale. Built-in token usage/cost is unavailable. The new body is for visual review, not installed or declared final.
