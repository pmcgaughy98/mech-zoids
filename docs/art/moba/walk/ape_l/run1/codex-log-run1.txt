# Walk playbook (walk lane)

How to make a machine's walk at 35 degrees, check it, and price it. Started 2026-11-05 by the walk lane ("Build mech
walk animations", Preston: "Get a walking animations session going that is skilled up from everything we learned").
Update this file when a lesson changes. The run log is `LOG.md` in this folder.

**Sources.** Read this session: `ANIMATION_PLAYBOOK.md` (all), `cam35_fleet/HANDOFF.md`, the rhino 35-degree walk
(`rhino_l/take-a/anim/walk35/`: REPORT.txt, clean/REPORT.txt, brief_run1.txt), the wolf walk4 run
(`wolf_m/take-b/anim/codex-walk4/REPORT.txt`, brief.txt, `common.txt`), the gorilla's set A walk report, the kits
(`docs/plan/moba-kits.md`), and the game feel lane's answers on speed (2026-11-05). General knowledge, not read
this session: the four key poses (Richard Williams, *The Animator's Survival Kit*), animal gait sequences
(Muybridge's plates, Hildebrand's gait studies), and the walk-to-run Froude rule (R. McNeill Alexander). Those
parts are marked as craft proposals. Nothing here is canon until Preston says so.

## 1. Rules that bind every walk
- **Build from the newest views.** Before any frames, look for `<take>/views_cam35_touchup/` and its APPROVED.txt.
  The rhino walk was drawn from `views_cam35` after Preston kept the touch-up, and needed `walk35_touchup.py` to
  recolour 40 frames afterwards.
- **The walk and the stills share one angle.** A machine's walk is drawn at 35 degrees only once its 35-degree stills
  are approved. Mixed angles pop at every start and stop.
- **Above the legs, every frame is the still**, moved only by the bob. Then idle to walk cannot pop. The rhino's
  35-degree walk matched its still on 0% differing pixels above the legs.
- **Legs are the still's own pixels, translated, never rotated or resampled.** Rotation left specks that cost a
  78,180-token cleanup on the rhino. Where a leg leans, step its parts 1 px a row.
- **Joints are redrawn, not pasted.** Pasted parts that did not join caused the cutoffs Preston rejected ("Too many
  cutoff etc."). Every joint and every join with the body is drawn in the still's colours and bands.
- **Feet stay planted** (section 7), and the drawn stride is checked against the game's speed (section 8).
- **No curled tails.** Tails lift and swing but stay straight, with the still's thickness and plate count.
- **Palette, canvas, offsets and feet row are the machine's own.** Keep its `palette.json`; place stills on the 192
  canvas at (+16, 160 - feet row).
- **Judge at 4x with the game's sel-out, the approved still in column 1.** He rejects "barely acceptable".
- **Never write into another lane's folders or the approved views.** This lane writes to `walk/<machine>/` only.
- **The session worktree has no `docs/art/moba`.** Write files in the worktree and copy them over with Bash (the
  fleet lane's method). C: was full on 2026-11-05, so the scratchpad was not usable.

## 2. Key poses (craft, proposal)
A walk is a controlled fall caught by each landing foot. Per foot, four poses:

| Pose | What happens | What the body does |
|---|---|---|
| Contact | The swinging foot lands ahead; the other foot of that pair is at the back of its stance | Hold the still's height |
| Down | Weight drops onto the landing foot | The dip: the body sinks 1 px (L can take 2); the shoulders or hips over that foot lead |
| Passing | The swinging foot passes the planted one, at its highest lift | The body returns to still height |
| Up | The planted foot pushes off | Body at still height (rising over it would lift the head off the still; not used yet) |

- On our machines the body only translates by whole pixels; armour does not squash. The rhino's approved bob is a
  1 px dip on f0 and f4 (the frames a fore foot lands).
- Head and tail lag the body by one frame (section 5). The rhino's pose-guided front walk leaned the head toward the
  planted foot, and Preston liked that walk.

| gorilla (L) | knuckle walk, diagonal pairs | right fist + left foot planted f0-f3, the other pair f4-f7; the fists swing as whole forearms | the set A walk (old angle) used diagonal pairs; the kit says "knuckle walking". Real gorillas use a four-beat walk: both are on the guide for Preston |

## 4. Weight and timing by size (proposal; the rhino, wolf and fennec numbers are real)
| Size | Frames | ms a frame | Cycle | Dip | Lifts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L | 8 | 125 (8 fps), rhino | 1.0 s | 1 px (2 px on a heavy landing, such as the gorilla dropping onto its fists) | 2-3 px |
| M | 8 | 100 (10 fps), wolf | 0.8 s | 1 px | 2-4 px |
| S | 8 | 100 (10 fps), fennec | 0.8 s | 0-1 px | 3-4 px |

Heavier means longer holds on the planted feet, a lower down pose and smaller lifts. Lighter means more air and a
quicker cycle. A cycle length is a placeholder until game feel and Preston set the speed (section 8).

## 5. Overlap and secondary motion
- **Tails:** straight. They trail the body bob by one frame and swing 1-2 px sideways, opposite the hips.
- **Ears, fins, antennae and cables:** trail by one frame, 1 px. Rigid armour plates move with their body part and
  never flex.
- **Gems, lenses and eyes:** carried with their part by translation, never redrawn smaller. Check them on every frame
  (the gorilla's hex gems broke in its hex_shield ne frames).
- **Arms that are legs** (gorilla fists, kangaroo gloves): the whole forearm block moves, and the shoulder join is
  redrawn.

## 6. Loops, frames and views
- **Seamless loop:** f7 to f0 is a normal step. Each foot does exactly one swing per cycle, the planted step is the
  same across the wrap, and the dip pattern repeats. Check it with an onion skin of f7 under f0.
- **Frames:** 8 a cycle, equal timing. Walks are retimed only with Preston's word (the wolf front and back were
  retimed into pairs on f0 and f4).
- **Views:** draw s, se, e, ne and n. The engine mirrors sw, w and nw, and a turn keeps the frame index.
- **Mirror phase check (new; a proposal for the engine):** a mirror swaps left and right, so in sw, w and nw the
  foot that is up is the other one. Turning from s to sw on frame 2 swaps which foot is up within one frame. For
  alternating gaits (walk, trot, biped), playing mirrored views at frame (i + 4) mod 8 keeps the same physical foot
  up, and the dip on f0 and f4 still lines up. Hops are symmetric, so their offset is 0. `walk_tools.py mirror`
  lists the foot that is up in all eight views.

## 7. Foot lock at 35 degrees
- A ground step of d texels along the body shows on screen as d x f x u. At pitch p and view yaw a (e 0, se 45,
  s 90), the screen vector is (cos a, sin a x sin p). At 35: e u (1, 0), f 1.000; se u (0.867, 0.497), f 0.815;
  s u (0, 1), f 0.574; ne and n the same with y negated.
- **Planted step a frame on the canvas = -P x f x u**, where P is the side-view step in px a frame. At P = 3:
  e (-3, 0); se (-2.12, -1.22); ne (-2.12, +1.22); s (0, -1.72); n (0, +1.72).
- **Lock rule:** the k-th planted frame's sole centre minus k planted steps stays within 2 px of the first. The rhino
  passed with e 0.00, se and ne 0.88, s and n 0.88 px.
- **Depth travel shows now.** At 35, a fore-and-back step in s and n shows at 0.574 of its length (0.16 at the old
  angle), so front and back walks need real foot travel, not reordered frames.
- **Toes toward the camera** may draw 2-3 px under the feet row (rhino s and n 163, se and ne 162). The floor hides
  none of it, and walk and idle both end on row 161 with sel-out.
- **The game camera is 30 degrees and the art is drawn at 35** (game feel, 2026-11-05). An s or n planted step drawn
  for 35 plays about 13% long in game: 0.22 px a frame at P = 3. That is under the 2 px lock gate; logged for game
  feel.

## 8. Stride against speed
- Drawn ground speed = P x fps x texel (m). Texel: L 0.020, M 0.018, S 0.016 m.
- Rhino: 3 x 8 x 0.020 = 0.48 m/s, kept by Preston ("Keep 0.48 m/s").
- The game: `move_speed = 3.5` m/s for every machine, a placeholder (`machine_test_player.gd:7`). `stride_speed`
  is one export for all machines (0.48), and `full_speed_walk_rate` is still 1.0, so the feet slide today.
- To plant at 3.5 m/s, a walk drawn at 0.48 m/s plays 7.3 times fast, or the side step must be 21.9 px a frame at
  L and 8 fps (24.3 at M, 27.3 at S). That is a run, not a walk: the gorilla's whole side view is 79 px long.
- **Natural walk speed (Froude, proposal):** v = sqrt(Fr x 9.81 x leg height in m). A comfortable walk is about
  Fr 0.25; animals switch to a run near Fr 0.5. `walk_tools.py stride` prints this per machine from its leg height.
- **Preston picks**, per game feel: lower move_speed, play the walk fast, draw longer strides, or add a run cycle.
  This lane never picks a speed. `stride_speed` needs a per-machine value once a second machine walks.

# Machine animation playbook

How to make a MOBA machine animation, or fix one, without relearning the rhino walk. This is what worked and

## 1. Preston's rules
- **The approved stills are the truth.** They are each machine's `<machine>/take-*/views/` (rhino `rhino_l/take-a`,

  Every frame is judged side by side against the still.
- **He rejects "barely acceptable".** Show a board with the approved still in column 1, and let him pick.
- **Feet stay planted.** Feet sit on the foot row in every frame, and a planted foot must not slide in the game.

  ("sel out only").
- **No curled tails** ("no curved tails as they are hard to rotate"). Tails lift and swing but stay straight.

## 4. Finishing, in this order
Most scripts are in `rhino_l/take-a/anim/`. Exceptions are named.

   - It maps Codex's own red eye through the exact finishing shrink and offset.
   - `se_eyes.py` tracks eyes into se frames from the nearest frame that has one (min score 0.55), and skips any
     frame where the fit is poor.
   - List every frame left without an eye for Preston. **Open:** rhino horn toss se f1 and f4, horn jab se f1, f3
     and f4.
2. **Broken parts (plates, gems, faces):** `ape_l/take-a/anim/back_plate.py`.
   - Cut the part from the still (its colours plus the ring they enclose), find it in each frame by matching the
     still's gem mask within 10 px, paste it there, then clear the box above it and dark texels stranded next to
     it.
   - Frames whose part already matches the still at 0.9 or better are left alone. Broken frames measured 0.76-0.80.
   - Cut with a square dilation (7 px) so corners such as the bezel's cap are included; a diamond cut them off.
3. **Shoulder line (rhino front):**
   - Geometry first: `retro-walk/pixellab/fix_front_shoulders.py` restores the still's shoulder plates.
   - `front_shoulder_ink.py` draws a domed dark arc over each knee-disc plate and lifts the texel under it to the
     highlight.

     that has it (match 0.76-0.87 on the braced shuffle).
4. **Detail ink:** `detail_ink.py` uses only the contour part of the Hollowtide detail-ink rule, in the machine's own
   darkest colour (`darkest()`). It brings a view up to the liked views' interior ink share and no further. The full
   rule (22-28%) was rejected.
   - **Gate with a margin:** ink a view only when its share is clearly below the target (under about 0.75 times).
     A view a point under the target doubled its ink and grew hard rings (the gorilla's n and ne trial).
5. **Seams:** each Codex set's `seam_fill.py` fills runs of 4+ transparent texels with ink on both sides. The
   originals go in `frames_pre_seam/`.
6. **Walk upper body (front, back):** `walk_to_still.py` puts the still's upper body, moved by each frame's bob,
   over the walk's own legs (section 9, step 7). Use it on any walk whose body drifts from the still.
7. **Idle:** `diag_idle.py` builds the idle from the approved still: the body above a cut row 13 px over the feet
   rises 0, 1, 1, 1, 0 px, the cut row is repeated to close the gap, and the feet never move.

   - **Views drawn from the game camera (35 degrees):** set the cut per view. Far feet sit up to about 30 rows above
     the feet row there, so a fixed 13-row cut lifts them. The rhino's cam35 idle cuts at s 152, se 131, e 141,

## 6. Speed: the drawn stride has to match the movement
- **Measure the stride:** `tools/debug/walk_stride_measure.py <frames dir> side --fps N` gives the speed a planted
  foot moves at the drawn frame rate.
- **The rhino walk:** planted feet move at 0.48 m/s, while the placeholder movement speed is 3.5 m/s. Unless the
  two match, the feet slide. Settled 2026-11-04, Preston: "Keep 0.48 m/s"; game feel plays the walk at
  `full_speed_walk_rate = move_speed / 0.48`.
- **The braced shuffle:** drawn by Codex from brace f3 (set D). The planted feet slide 2 px a frame, which is
  0.40 m/s at 10 fps; braced movement is 1.225 m/s, so the feet slide about 3x. Open as of 2026-11-04: slow the
  brace to 0.4 m/s or accept the slide. Preston picks.
- **The fix options:**
  - slow the move;
  - raise the playback rate (`speed_scale`, `full_speed_walk_rate`);
  - draw a longer stride or a faster gait.
- **Engine rules that hide seams:**
  - Turning mid-step keeps the step: the new view picks up at the same frame.
  - A held action (brace) plays its `<action>_shuffle_<view>` while moving, and plants the held frame again on stop.

## 9. The rhino walk, start to finish
**The rule to carry forward:** for a straight front or back walk on a four-legged machine, take the gait from an
approved side walk and pose it frame by frame. Don't prompt for it. Then put the still's upper body back over the
posed legs (step 8).

1. **Side and se were the easy part.** Retro Diffusion's walking style gave side and se walks that read as walks; the
   legs are spread apart from those angles.
2. **Straight front and back failed five times:** Codex twice (walk A weak, walk B floating 6-7 px with mismatched
   pairs and shortening legs), Retro twice, and text-prompted PixelLab once (it grew about 9 px taller mid-step and

   together, stretched them, or invented parts. `walk_frontback.py` (a bob from the side row, whole-leg lifts in

   - Map the front legs to the skeleton's arms and the back legs to its legs.

def comps(mask, conn=N8):
    lab = np.zeros(mask.shape, int)
    n = 0
    sizes = {}
    for y, x in zip(*np.nonzero(mask)):
        if lab[y, x]:
            continue
        n += 1
        st = [(y, x)]
        lab[y, x] = n
        c = 0
        while st:
            cy, cx = st.pop()
            c += 1
            for dy, dx in conn:
                ny, nx = cy + dy, cx + dx
                if 0 <= ny < mask.shape[0] and 0 <= nx < mask.shape[1] and mask[ny, nx] and not lab[ny, nx]:
                    lab[ny, nx] = n
                    st.append((ny, nx))
        sizes[n] = c
    return lab, sizes

def view_vec(view, pitch):
    """Screen px per side-view px of travel along the body axis (= f * u)."""
    a, p = math.radians(YAW_DEG[view]), math.radians(pitch)
    return (math.cos(a), math.sin(a) * math.sin(p))


def foot_path(start, P, lifts):
    """(offset along body in side px, lift px) per frame. Matches the rhino's paths at P 3, lifts 2,3,2."""
    out = []
    for f in range(8):
        k = (f - start) % 8
        if k < 3:
            out.append((-2 * P + (k + 1) * P, lifts[k]))
        else:
            out.append((2 * P - (k - 3) * P, 0))
    return out


def swap_lr(name):

def load_rgba(path, offset=(0, 0), canvas=None):
    im = Image.open(path).convert('RGBA')
    if canvas and (tuple(offset) != (0, 0) or im.size != (canvas, canvas)):
        c = Image.new('RGBA', (canvas, canvas), (0, 0, 0, 0))
        c.paste(im, tuple(offset))
        im = c
    return np.array(im)


def bottom_profile(alpha):
    prof = np.full(alpha.shape[1], -1)
    for x in range(alpha.shape[1]):
        ys = np.nonzero(alpha[:, x])[0]
        if len(ys):
            prof[x] = ys.max()
    return prof


def count_pieces(alpha):
    H, W = alpha.shape
    seen = np.zeros((H, W), bool)
    sizes = []
    for y0, x0 in zip(*np.nonzero(alpha)):
        if seen[y0, x0]:
            continue
        stack, size = [(y0, x0)], 0
        seen[y0, x0] = True
        while stack:
            y, x = stack.pop()
            size += 1
            for dy in (-1, 0, 1):
                for dx in (-1, 0, 1):
                    yy, xx = y + dy, x + dx
                    if 0 <= yy < H and 0 <= xx < W and alpha[yy, xx] and not seen[yy, xx]:
                        seen[yy, xx] = True
                        stack.append((yy, xx))
        sizes.append(size)
    return sorted(sizes, reverse=True)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- soles
def sole_segments(alpha, tol=1):
    prof = bottom_profile(alpha)
    segs, cur = [], []
    for x in range(len(prof)):
        if prof[x] < 0:
            if cur:
                segs.append(cur)
                cur = []
            continue
        if cur and abs(int(prof[x]) - int(prof[cur[-1]])) > tol:
            segs.append(cur)
            cur = []
        cur.append(x)
    if cur:
        segs.append(cur)
    out = []
    for s in segs:
        low = max(int(prof[x]) for x in s)
        cols = [x for x in s if prof[x] >= low - tol]
        out.append({'x0': min(cols), 'x1': max(cols), 'row': low, 'seg': [s[0], s[-1]]})
    return out

def build_targets(feet, gait_name, P, fps, size, lifts, pitch):
    gait = GAITS[gait_name]
    paths = {foot: foot_path(start, P, lifts) for foot, start in gait.items()}
    fore = [f for f in gait if f.endswith('F')] or list(gait)
    dips = sorted({(gait[f] + 3) % 8 for f in fore})
    t = {'P_side_px': P, 'fps': fps, 'size': size, 'planted_pace_m_s': round(P * fps * TEXEL_M[size], 3),
         'gait_name': gait_name, 'gait': gait, 'lifts': lifts, 'pitch': pitch, 'dip_frames': dips,
         'paths': {k: [list(p) for p in v] for k, v in paths.items()}, 'views': {}}
    for view, vfeet in feet['views'].items():
        vx, vy = view_vec(view, pitch)
        f = math.hypot(vx, vy)
        vt = {'u': [round(vx / f, 4), round(vy / f, 4)], 'f': round(f, 4),
              'planted_step_px': [round(-P * vx, 3), round(-P * vy, 3)], 'visible_feet': {}}
        for foot, (x0, x1, row) in vfeet.items():
            cx = (x0 + x1) / 2
            frames = []
            for i, (off, lift) in enumerate(paths[foot]):
                frames.append({'frame': i, 'offset': off, 'lift': lift,
                               'sole_centre': [round(cx + off * vx, 2), round(row + off * vy - lift, 2)]})
            vt['visible_feet'][foot] = {'still_sole': [x0, x1, row], 'frames': frames}
        t['views'][view] = vt
    return t

    img.paste(s, (0, 1 if frame in dips else 0), s)
    img = img.resize((W * scale, H * scale), Image.NEAREST)
    d = ImageDraw.Draw(img)

## 7. Foot lock at 35 degrees

def measure_sole(alpha, cx, cy, w, side=3, up=6, down=4):
    H, W = alpha.shape
    xa, xb = max(0, int(math.floor(cx - w / 2 - side))), min(W - 1, int(math.ceil(cx + w / 2 + side)))
    ya, yb = max(0, int(cy) - up), min(H - 1, int(cy) + down)
    lows = {}
    for x in range(xa, xb + 1):
        ys = np.nonzero(alpha[ya:yb + 1, x])[0]
        if len(ys):
            lows[x] = ya + int(ys.max())
    if not lows:
        return None
    seed = min(lows, key=lambda x: abs(lows[x] - cy) + 0.5 * abs(x - cx))
    run = [seed]
    for step in (-1, 1):
        x = seed
        while (x + step) in lows and abs(lows[x + step] - lows[x]) <= 1 and lows[x + step] >= lows[seed] - 2:
            x += step
            run.append(x)
    row = max(lows[x] for x in run)
    cols = [x for x in run if lows[x] >= row - 1]
    return ((min(cols) + max(cols)) / 2, row)

The MOBA creatures follow the Hollowtide sprite canon, where the outline comes from the sel-out shader
(hollowtide docs/art/crew-dungeon-selout-2026-10-10/candidate/scripts/arena/sel_out.gdshaderinc) instead of being
painted. This game has no such shader, so its creature rules are applied here per pixel, exactly:
  1. a transparent pixel whose up, down, left or right neighbour is a light or material colour (luma 0.23 or more)
     becomes the outline colour: one texel of outline outside the silhouette;
  2. a silhouette edge pixel (a transparent pixel up, down, left or right of it) darker than luma 0.23 is darkened
     to the outline colour (per channel minimum), so existing dark edges get no extra thickness;
  3. the shader's speck cleanup only merges colours closer than 0.12 with luma over 0.16; no two rhino colours
     are that close, so it changes nothing here and is left out; its detail-ink mode is off for creatures.
Outline colour: the rhino palette's darkest, #141218 (the shader's #0B0E11 would add a 13th colour; they differ by
under 10 per channel). The feet still stand on y=160; under a light foot edge the outline sits on y=161.
Reads every sheet's frames/ and writes frames_selout/ beside it, so rerun after any fix to the frames.
"""
from pathlib import Path

import numpy as np
from PIL import Image, ImageDraw

ANIM = Path(__file__).parent
OUTLINE = np.array([0x14, 0x12, 0x18], np.uint8)
SHEETS = [ANIM / "codex-set-a" / s for s in ("idle", "walk", "horn-jab", "horn-toss")] + \
         [ANIM / "codex-set-b" / s for s in ("brace", "stampede", "death")] + [ANIM / "codex-ground-pound/clean"] + \
         [ANIM / "codex-set-c" / s for s in ("stampede", "horn-toss", "ground-pound", "horn-jab")] + \
         [ANIM / "codex-set-d" / s for s in ("brace", "death", "idle", "brace-shuffle")]  # se/ne rows, shuffle


def selout(a):
    op = a[..., 3] > 127
    luma = (a[..., :3].astype(float) / 255) @ np.array([0.2126, 0.7152, 0.0722])
    light = op & (luma >= 0.23)

    def nb(m, fill):
        p = np.pad(m, 1, constant_values=fill)
        return p[:-2, 1:-1], p[2:, 1:-1], p[1:-1, :-2], p[1:-1, 2:]

    repair = ~op & np.logical_or.reduce(nb(light, False))
    edge = op & np.logical_or.reduce([~x for x in nb(op, False)])
    dark_edge = edge & (luma < 0.23)
    out = a.copy()
    out[dark_edge, :3] = np.minimum(a[dark_edge, :3], OUTLINE)
    out[repair, :3] = OUTLINE
    out[repair, 3] = 255
    return out, int(repair.sum()), int(dark_edge.sum())


def main():

    for d in SHEETS:
        src, dst = d / "frames", d / "frames_selout"
        dst.mkdir(exist_ok=True)

            a = np.asarray(Image.open(p).convert("RGBA"))
            b, added, darkened = selout(a)
