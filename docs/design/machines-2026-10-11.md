# Machine designs (proposals for Preston, 2026-10-11)

Preston: "make the weapons and the way their machine is gonna look and operate. Don't copy anything from Zoids,
colors too." Birds: avoid the famous red one and the white one, a different shade or kind of the colour is fine.
"I want the gorilla to be green"; the Zoids gorilla has missiles on its shoulders and back, so ours pivots
away from missiles, but light weapons on a gorilla "would look wack": so heavy, close-in, thrown weight.

Nothing here is canon. Names are working labels. Data: `scripts/data/machines.gd`. Shapes and angles: the
view guides in `docs/team/view-guides/<id>.png` (eight views each). Codex redraws all four from these.

## The house rules for every machine
- **Real animal, then machine.** Each design starts from how the animal actually moves and hunts, and every
  weapon sits where that animal's own weapon is (paws, jaw, talons, fists). That is what makes it ours.
- **Own colours per machine, faction in the lights.** Player machines have teal eyes and running lights
  (`#5fe1d0`), rivals amber (`#ffb02e`). Frames are shared gunmetal (`#4a4f57`, `#2b2e33`).
- **Steer clear of known Zoids looks** (checked against general knowledge and a quick search; check any new
  design the same way): no white-and-blue lion, no blades folding off a lion's back, no red-and-black or
  black-and-purple raptor with a chest cannon, no plain red or plain white bird, no gorilla with shoulder or
  back missile racks.

## Tiger type (`lion_m`, player starter, M, ground)
- **Colours:** saffron `#e0a33a` body, charcoal `#2a2c30` bands across the back (the stripes are armour
  plates), verdigris `#3f8f84` tail fin, teal lights.
- **How it moves:** prowls low with the head down; the banded plates flex like a spine when it runs; the
  tail is a stabilizer fin that whips to turn hard. No mane, no saber fangs.
- **Weapons:** Rake Claws (front paws, three ceramic claws each, swiped low); Jaw Gun (a short twin barrel
  slung under the lower jaw, fires as the head drops); Spring Pounce (hind-leg pistons fire it forward,
  claws first).

## Raptor type (`raptor_s`, rival, S, ground)
- **Colours:** deep plum `#5b2a4e`, bone `#d9cfb6` belly and jaw line, bronze `#8c6a3f` tail pod, amber lights.
- **How it moves:** runs on two legs with the body level and the tail out straight as a counterweight;
  quick head bobs (its sensors). A scout, light and twitchy.
- **Weapons:** Snap Jaw (a quick sideways bite); Tail Pod (a four-cell launcher at the tail tip, fired with a
  tail whip); Sickle Leap (jumps and lands on the toe sickles).

## Hawk type (`hawk_s`, rival, S, flyer)
- **Colours:** oxide red `#8f3324` (rust, not bright red), slate `#3c4f57` wings, brass `#c08a3e` beak and
  talons, amber lights.
- **How it moves:** never hovers; it banks in wide circles. The flight feathers are louvred vanes that open to
  brake and shut to dive.
- **Weapons:** Grapple Talons (grab, lift, drop); Root Guns (two guns in the wing roots, fired on a strafing
  pass); Stoop (wings folded, a straight drop from height).

## Gorilla type (`ape_l`, new, player, L, ground)
- **Colours:** moss green `#4a7a3c`, dark pine `#2c4a2a` head and back blocks, bronze `#a8763e` fists and
  chest plate, teal lights.
- **How it moves:** knuckle-walks on armoured fists; rears up on two legs to slam. The chest is a thick
  bulwark plate it raises when braced (ties to the Brace command already in the game).
- **Weapons (heavy, no missiles):** Piston Knuckles (the fists it walks on are hydraulic rams); Ballast Throw
  (tears a block off the ballast rack on its back and hurls it in an arc); Quake Slam (both fists into the
  ground, the shockwave runs to the target).
- **An idea, not built:** the ballast rack is armour and ammo at once. Each throw makes it lighter and a
  little faster, but easier to hurt.
- **Draft numbers:** health 190 (x1.3 for size L), energy 50, speed 22 (x0.85), defense 30. Not yet in a battle.
