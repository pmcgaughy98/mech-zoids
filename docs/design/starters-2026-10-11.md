# The four starters (synthesis and proposals, 2026-10-11)

Preston: the raptor, tiger and hawk are too mainstream; the player picks between **four starter animals**; the
**green gorilla stays**; a **bird stays but not an eagle or anything from Zoids**, and pivot away from red. Use
synthesis and the connectors (Retro Game Gather, Director's Lookbook, Firecrawl). Nothing here is canon.

## What was researched
- **Firecrawl, Zoids Wiki "List of Zoids"** (422 entries, 149 animal types; read 2026-10-11): lions 16,
  tyrannosaurs 15, velociraptors 8, tigers 7, gorillas 7, wolves 6. Birds used: eagle, hawk, falcon, owl,
  duck, ostrich, peafowl, phoenix, archaeopteryx, generic bird of prey, plus pterosaurs (pteranodon,
  pterodactyl, quetzalcoatlus). Never used: shoebill, pangolin, hippo (and heron, hornbill, kingfisher,
  armadillo, otter, giant salamander, among others).
- **Retro Game Gather** (identified, with their external pages, for the starter-choice structure): Zoids Saga
  and Zoids Saga II / Legacy (GBA, Tomy), Medarot 2 Kabuto and Kuwagata (GBC), Custom Robo GX (GBA), Robopon 2
  Ring and Cross (GBA), Pokemon Ruby (GBA).
- **Director's Lookbook** palette cards: Blue Hour Cool, Ochre Madder and Delft, Desert Dusk (hexes below).

## Structure taken from finished games (general knowledge of the games above, not their data)
- Starters differ first by **silhouette** at a glance and then by **how they fight**, not by stats alone:
  Pokemon's three cover a counter triangle; Medarot's two versions split melee and ranged.
- Four choices work when each maps to a **different system** the player will meet: here the game already has
  three bodies (ground, flyer, swimmer) and four weapon types (melee, shell, missile, beam).
- A starter shows its personality in one move the others can't do.

**Alternatives named** (swap any one): bird: hornbill (the casque as a ram) or kingfisher (diving spear);
defender: armadillo; swimmer: giant river otter or giant salamander.
