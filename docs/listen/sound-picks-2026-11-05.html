Sound picks

Two picks, about a minute. All free: synthesised from scratch or built from the game's own sounds. Loudness is matched, so no option wins by being louder. I can't hear audio, so my picks come from the design; your ears decide.

1. Rhino ground pound

Each clip: the rear-up, a beat, then the slam (the slam also ends the stampede).

A. In the game now: servo climb, then thump, crunch, hull clang and rumble.

B. Steel: machine first. Hydraulics and a climbing servo, then a deep thump and a big ringing metal clang.

C. Quake: ground first. Rumble building, then a sharp crack, a heavy boom and gravel raining down, with a little metal.

My pick: C. The callout is "Ground's coming up!", so the earth breaking should lead.

2. Wall muffling

Each clip: a tower fight in the open, then the same fight behind a wall, over the lane ambience.

None (today): a fight behind a wall sounds the same as one in front of you.

Light: duller and a bit quieter. You still hear it and can place it; it sounds around a corner.

Heavy: a low thud only. Clearly hidden, but hard to follow a fight you can't see.

My pick: Light. A fight behind a wall is still useful to hear; light keeps it readable and still makes walls matter.

How to answer

Something like "pound C, muffle light". If no pound option is good enough, a paid take (ElevenLabs sound effects) is the next step, priced first. Made by tools/audio/sound_picks.py.
