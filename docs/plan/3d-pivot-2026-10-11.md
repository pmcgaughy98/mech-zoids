Preston: hand-drawn machines in eight views won't stay consistent, so the game goes **3D**: one model per machine,
seen from any angle, no eight-view drawing. "Figure out how to make mech 3D models that look legit." The
multi-view sprite wiring (`docs/plan/multi-view-machines.md`) stays as a fallback; a 3D model can also export a
turntable sheet if 2D views are ever needed.

## What makes it look legit (synthesis to do properly; general knowledge so far)
Custom Robo, Armored Core and the 3D Zoids games: a strong silhouette, chunky separate parts, panel lines and decals
painted into the textures, a small palette per machine, and cel shading with an ink outline and rim light in Godot.
Armored Core builds machines from swappable parts, which fits the gorilla's ballast rack and later customization.

## Rig, walk, and the rebuild toward Preston's sprite (Claude, 2026-10-11, late)
- Rig: 14 bones, every part rides one bone rigidly (`bone_for()`), so it moves like a machine. `knuckle_walk()` is
  a 1.5 s in-place loop timed from gorilla gait data (Ito et al. 2025, Scientific Reports: diagonal sequence, about
  65-67% ground contact per limb, hind foot lands about half a cycle before the hand on that side, stiff arms).
  Exported in the GLB; the Godot lab plays it and the check confirms it runs and the arm swings.
- Finish: edge wear baked from curvature (pointiness) into the atlas; the paint's light-to-dark spread went from
  0.10 to 0.15 and later about 0.24.
- GokaiOh (megazord) study: colour structure measured (black frame about 31%, hero colour 16%, gold about 20%,
  white/silver about 15%, vivid). A lime/white hero scheme was tried and then dropped when Preston gave the target.
- Target is now Preston's own sprite (three views; the rear three-quarter is
  `docs/art/ape-rd-2026-10-11/source_preston_pick.webp`). Palette taken from it: greens 3f6134/598643/84a867,
  orange b67336, greys 3a3a3c/656368, near-black 1c1e20. `build()` was rewritten to its layout: head front and
  highest with a stacked crest, orange chest shield, wide shoulders, three capped blocks across the back, a
  segmented back over a dark core, long splayed arms on huge orange fists, short bent legs with orange claws.
  About 9,800 triangles, 2.9 m tall, 3.4 m across the fists.
- The Godot lab light was calibrated so the rendered green matches the sprite's greens (sun 0.2, fill 0.6).
- Side by side: `docs/art/3d/ape_l_v3_vs_sprite.png`. Still short of the sprite: fewer, bigger plates (the sprite
  has many small distinct plates with dark seams), the crest reads as steps, the hind legs should splay more, the
