# The Four Lines: the world, built out (2026-11-17)

Preston picked direction B, 2026-11-17: "B". The three directions are in `lore-world-2026-11-17.md`.

**Nothing here is canon.** Every name is a proposal for you to rename. No kit changes: each legend is built from the
Power and Rig lines in `moba-kits.md`, and stays inside what each kit left out.

## The world in short

- **Machines move like animals because of the Recordings.** Wildlife film units (the chameleon's rig) recorded how
  animals move, and engineers learned to run machines on those recordings. A recording is a movement pattern, like a
  gait. It has no mind, and the machines are not alive.
- **Working machines came first:** thumpers, tugs, locomotives, catapults, salvage haulers, pile drivers, seeding
  planes, rescue snakes, pursuit units. Their cores only gave power.
- **Four accidents at work made the first power cores,** and each grew into a **line**: a school of engineers who
  build and tune one family of tech. **Pressure** (tremor, steam, cavitation), **Field** (gravity, magnetism, shields,
  kinetic, bonds), **Sky** (storm, moonlight, signal, cloaking) and **Phase** (gates, pass-through, spectral).
- **Cores are tuned at the foundries,** old plants run by the overseer, which is older than any line. A plant can be
  tuned to one line at a time. So the lines fight to hold plants.
- **A match is one of those fights.** Each team is a crew under contract to a line for that day. Crews hire machines
  from every line, so teams are mixed and the same machine can be on both sides. Each crew brings a tuning core in
  its line's tech, and the overseer tunes the plant to whichever core is left standing. So destroying the other
  crew's core hands the plant to your line. The overseer logs it all as a shift, as it logs everything, so its lines
  stay exactly as they are.
- **Nobody is the villain, but everyone fears the Phase line.** Its first gate opened inside a foundry and stayed open
  for nine days.

**Decided:** the six machines with no rig (gorilla, hippo, hummingbird, fennec, shoebill, squirrel) were **built by
their line for fighting, with no working past**. Preston, 2026-11-18: "Yes, line-built".

## The four lines

| Line | Its tech | Founded by | Home ground | What it wants | What the others say about it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pressure** | waves through rock, water and steam | the rhino: "The Pulse That Came Back" | mines, rail yards, sea walls, pile fields | more plants, because heavy cores burn out and need retuning most often | the oldest and biggest line; most crews started in it |
| **Field** | push, pull, hold | the lobster: "The Night the Chains Broke" | harbours, salvage yards, launch decks | plants that stay put; a Field tune holds longest | steady and stubborn; "Field holds" is said of anyone who won't give ground |
| **Sky** | light, weather, signal | the falcon: "The Storm That Kept Raining Charge" | airfields, relay masts, marsh and high ground | to keep the Recordings, which every machine runs on, and license them to the others | they know where everyone is, and nobody trusts what they can't see |
| **Phase** | making solid matter and space give way | the cobra: "The Collapse" | tunnels and sealed workshops | a plant of its own; no line lets a plant stay tuned to Phase for long | feared since the Open Gate, and still hired by everyone |

**Lines are told apart by a mark, not a colour,** because each power keeps its own colour on either team (the
gorilla's green, the bison's magma orange). Designing the marks is an art task; none is proposed here.

**Alliances shift, but some lean one way.** Pressure and Field are old partners (rail and harbour). Sky and Phase are
rivals: one sees everything, the other goes where it can't be seen. Everyone needs Sky's Recordings, and resents it.

## Key events, in order

1. **The Recordings.** The film units record animals moving. Working machines are built on the recordings.
2. **The Working Age.** Machines do the hard jobs. The overseer already runs the foundries that make and service
   cores; nobody's records say who built the plants. (A mystery left open on purpose.)
3. **The Pulse That Came Back** (rhino). Pressure is founded.
4. **The Night the Chains Broke** (lobster). Field is founded.
5. **The Storm That Kept Raining Charge** (falcon). Sky is founded, and claims the Recordings as its own.
6. **The Collapse** (cobra). Phase is founded, as heroes.
7. **The First Hold.** The lines learn that the overseer retunes a whole plant to the last tuning core left standing
   in it. The first fight over a plant. Every match since is a hold.
8. **The Open Gate** (fennec). Phase's first gate opens inside a foundry and stays open for nine days. Machines and
   parts go through and come out somewhere else, or don't come out. Phase goes from heroes to feared.
9. **The Recovery** (wolf). Pursuit units fitted with phase chambers hunt down what came out of the gate.
10. **The Last Freight** (bison). Field and Sky cut the rail one winter; the Pressure line's plant runs its core on
    what one locomotive can haul through a blizzard.
11. **Now.** The lines hold some plants and fight over the rest. Crews hire across lines.
12. **Coming.** Ice, wind, fire and shadow join the four lines, and a fifth line starts growing (see the hooks below).

Most of these happen away from the foundries, so the world isn't boxed into matches (the League of Legends lesson in
the directions doc).

## The sixteen machines

"Rig" is the job it did before its line refitted it. "Line-built" means no working past, as decided above.

| Machine | Line | Origin | Legend |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rhino** | Pressure | seismic thumper | **The Pulse That Came Back.** A survey thumper's pulse came back from deep rock stronger than it went in, and the pistons caught it. The first tremor core still stores what it blocks. |
| **Hippo** | Pressure | line-built | **The Full Tank.** Pressure's answer to the First Hold: a machine that brings its own pressure into a fight, in three windowed tanks. Its one animal trait is the jaws. |
| **Bison** | Pressure | steam locomotive | **The Last Freight.** It plowed snow off the line and hauled the plant's supplies home on its coupling chains. |
| **Pistol shrimp** | Pressure | pile driver | **One Strike.** A slow lift, one drop, and the water around the ram flashed white before the sound arrived. The first cavitation core: all of it in one strike, nothing kept back. |
| **Lobster** | Field | anchor-handling tug | **The Night the Chains Broke.** In a storm its anchor chains snapped, and the tug held a drilling rig in place by weight alone. The first gravity field. |
| **Pangolin** | Field | magnetic salvage hauler | **The Wreck That Moved.** It was stripping plating off a wreck when the loose plates slid the last few metres to it on their own. Crews called it the wreck that moved, but only the plates did. The first magnetic core works like a scrapyard lifting magnet: it grips whatever it touches and anything loose close by, and fades within a few metres. It can tear the plates off a machine and steer itself onto one, but it can't lift one. |
| **Gorilla** | Field | line-built | **The Green Wall.** In the First Hold, its hex shields held a foundry gate for the Field line. The shields have been green ever since, on whoever fields one. |
| **Kangaroo** | Field | launch catapult | **The Held Launch.** A deck took a hit mid-launch, and the crew held the catapult. The stored launch energy had nowhere to go, and the core learned to let it out in short bursts. The first kinetic core. |
| **Hummingbird** | Field | line-built | **The Tether.** Field built it to hold its own machines together: a link beam is a small field between two machines. Its trait is flutter; it hovers low and never walks. |
| **Cobra** | Phase | rescue snake robot | **The Collapse.** A tunnel came down on a crew. The rescue snake's core, pushed past its limit in the rubble, made the rock go soft, and it brought them out. The first pass-through core, and the founding of Phase. |
| **Fennec** | Phase | line-built | **The Open Gate.** Phase built it to carry gates, and its first gate would not close. It carries no animal trait: its gates do its moving. |
| **Wolf** | Phase | pursuit unit with a capture launcher | **The Recovery.** After the Open Gate, Phase fitted pursuit units with red phase chambers to track down and catch what came through. They still hire out as hunters. |
| **Falcon** | Sky | cloud-seeding plane | **The Storm That Kept Raining Charge.** A seeding run seeded a storm that never stopped. The first ion storm, and the founding of Sky. |
| **Shoebill** | Sky | line-built | **The Long Watch.** Sky built it to watch over a held plant all night, its eye lenses gathering moonlight. Its patience shows as moon phases: crescent, half, full. |
| **Squirrel** | Sky | line-built | **The Relay.** When the Open Gate cut every signal in the district, couriers carried word mast to mast, hopping and switching direction, their tails working as antennas. |
| **Chameleon** | Sky | wildlife film unit | **The First Recordings.** The film units watched animals without being seen and made the recordings every machine runs on. Its cloaking grew out of the old film hides. |

<details>
<summary>Checked against other worlds, so it stays ours</summary>

- **Four nations with four elements** (the best-known example is Avatar: The Last Airbender) is the nearest neighbour
  to B. Kept apart: the lines are engineering schools, not nations or peoples; their powers are machine hardware, not
  a gift anyone is born with; and fire, ice and wind join existing lines instead of getting a line each, so no line is
  an element.
- **League of Legends:** its regions are places and peoples. The lines are trades that span places. Never "hextech".
- **Zoids:** the machines are not alive (a recording is a gait, not a mind), there is no planet name, and no two
  nations at war.
- **Horizon:** people built these machines; the overseer only runs plants and makes minions. No apocalypse.
- **Deadlock:** no patron per team, no ritual, no wish. One overseer, which doesn't care who wins.
- **SMITE:** no god or myth from it. Odes stay kit themes only.
