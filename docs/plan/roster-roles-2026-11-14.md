# Roster roles: what each machine does to a match, and who comes next (2026-11-14)

Preston, 2026-11-14: "what roles for our mecha beasts for moba do we have? Not just meaning what class they would
normally pick but like what they're impact on the game and kit/abilities are suppoused to work ... then lets make a
potential creature lineup for future creatures if I want to make mroe."

**Sources:** the roster and every kit in `moba-kits.md` (all 15 settled as of 2026-11-14); the four-layer rule, the map
ability kinds and the "New animals" list in `kit-design-2026-11-11.md`; Preston's picks for the eleven creatures that
already have sheets, in `power-body-recreations.md`. The class words (vanguard, warden, diver and so on) are common MOBA genre terms from
general knowledge, not checked against a source in this pass. Odes stay themes only (Preston, 2026-11-12).

## 1. The 15 today

| Machine | Size | Class (MOBA term) | Its impact on a match | How the kit does it | It wins when | It loses to |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rhino | L | tank: **vanguard** (engage) | starts the fight and drags the front line forward | front plating and Brace block hits and store Seismic charge; ground pound slows a circle; Tremor charge carries a target into its team | it lands Tremor charge on a carry | kiting, hits from the side, the lobster's Anchor (no carries) |
| Gorilla | L | tank: **warden** (protector) | keeps its carries alive | silverback aura, hex shields that clear a slow or root, a taunt, a leap to an ally, the hex dome that traps enemies inside | divers jump its back line | long-range poke that never walks in (shoebill, falcon) |
| Hippo | L | tank: **all-in** (juggernaut style) | turns the fight into a brawl in its pools | Pressure Tanks fill by fighting and overcharge the next skill; Vent puddle; Piston Drop stun; Lockjaw drag; Toxic Flood lake | the fight stays in its pools | being kited before the tanks fill |
| Bison | L | tank: **catcher who plays back** | catches whoever steps up and hauls them into its team | Coupling Chain then Haul (a buffer-slam stun), Whistle Stop calls the team in, Plow throws a coupled enemy to the chain's end, Freight hauls a line of enemies to a pile-up stun | someone steps up in chain range | a dodged chain, a stun on it mid-haul |
| Lobster | L | support: **catcher** | pulls one target out of position and keeps its own team from being moved | Weight rings slow running away, claw grab pulls, pinch roots, Anchor stops knocks, pulls and carries, gravity well | a carry steps into grab range | flankers, shields on the target, a gate out |
| Kangaroo | M | fighter: **duelist** (skirmisher) | wins one-on-ones and cuts a target off | rhythm stacks, hop to friend or foe, tail kick into walls, guard counter, title bout ring | it gets a carry alone | crowd control before the ring; open ground with no walls |
| Pangolin | M | fighter: **diver** | gets into the back line and stays alive there | salvage steals armour, rail dive to magnetised enemies, scale flare, iron ball | it reaches the back line with stolen armour | roots and slows, burst before the dive |
| Wolf | M | assassin: **executioner** | finishes the wounded and punishes a split team | scent through fog, Shade Step, Snare, Shade Pack (team speed), run down (missing-health bite, heals on a kill) | a team splits or retreats hurt | a grouped team, shields and heals |
| Cobra | M | jungler: **ganker / route opener** | arrives from where there is no path | Wall Line over inner walls, Ghost Run, Hood Gaze stun, Seam (unseen inside a wall), Breach | the enemy forgets the walls | reveals near walls, tanks who face it |
| Shoebill | M | mage: **artillery** | pokes from very far and traps the approach | moon phases (stand still to charge), moonbeam line, moon pool trap, wane hop, moonlance | it gets to stand still | divers and anything that dashes onto it |
| Fennec | S | mage: **utility / route** | moves its whole team in, out and across the map | gate echo, two-faced shot, pocket gates, far gates, great gate with rift boom | its team turns fights with numbers | a catch before the gate opens |
| Falcon | M | ranged carry: **range marksman** | steady damage that grows with distance | keen eye, Ion Lance pierce, Seed Flare, stoop dodge, Seeding Run | it fights at full range | divers, assassins, gap closers |
| Squirrel | S | ranged carry: **mobile** | kites, spreads damage, scouts | Hearsay bounce darts, Branch Skip, Crosstalk spread, Tail Ping reveal, Broadcast burst window | it kites a slow team | hard catches (lobster, bison) |
| Chameleon | M | support: **scout / stealth** | sees first and hides the team | Long Lens, Bent Ray round corners, camouflage, colour flash blind, shared camouflage | ambushes and escapes | reveals (squirrel, wolf, fennec) |
| Hummingbird | S | support: **healer** (enchanter) | keeps one ally topped up and charms divers | Kindred link heals over time, Link needle, Surge line speed, Flutter, Kindred ring heal and charm | its carry survives a long fight | burst; anti-heal, once one exists |

Sizes: 3 small, 7 medium, 5 large. Roles: 4 tanks, 2 fighters, 1 assassin, 1 jungler, 2 mages, 2 ranged carries,
3 supports.

**Who has invisibility today:** the chameleon (camouflage, and shared camouflage for its team) and the cobra (unseen
only while inside a wall, during Seam). No assassin has it.

## 2. The jobs a team needs, and who does them

| Job | Covered by | Depth |
|---|---|---|
| Engage (start the fight) | rhino, hippo, bison, pangolin, kangaroo (one target), lobster (gravity well) | strong |
| Protect / peel | gorilla, lobster Anchor, hummingbird, cobra Hood Gaze | fine |
| Catch (pick one) | lobster, bison, hippo Lockjaw, wolf Snare, shoebill moon pool | **pile** |
| Heal allies | hummingbird only | **thin** |
| Shield allies | gorilla only | thin |
| Buff allies | bison Whistle Stop (team speed), wolf Shade Pack, hummingbird Surge line | thin |
| Zone (deny an area) | hippo pools, rhino tremble, bison slag, shoebill moon pool | fine |
| Burst (delete one) | wolf, kangaroo title bout, shoebill moonlance | fine |
| Steady ranged damage | falcon, squirrel | thin |
| Poke from range | shoebill, falcon, fennec | fine |
| Take towers (siege) | nobody does it better than anyone else | **gap** |
| Move the team (routes) | fennec gates, cobra Breach, wolf Shade Pack, hummingbird Surge line | fine |
| Reveal | squirrel, wolf, falcon, chameleon, fennec | **pile** |
| Stealth | chameleon (support), cobra Seam (walls only) | thin; no stealth assassin |
| Stop displacement | lobster Anchor | thin, fine as is |
| Cut healing (anti-heal) | nobody | **gap**, matters once there are two healers |
| Summons, turrets, drones | nobody | **gap** (a specialist) |
| Duel one-on-one | kangaroo | fine |
| Silence | nobody since 2026-11-05 (the fennec's doors of war left) | a note, not a must |

**Piles to keep from growing:** reveals (5), catchers (5), knockbacks (kangaroo, gorilla, hippo, bison, pangolin,
cobra, fennec), and three "pressure" machines once the shark and pistol shrimp exist (hippo, shark, pistol shrimp).
`kit-design-2026-11-11.md` already keeps those three apart by verb and colour: fill and spend in violet, currents in
aqua, burst in white.

**The roster with these (15 + 11 = 26):** 4 tanks, 3 fighters, 1 assassin, 2 junglers, 4 mages, 6 ranged carries,
5 supports, and the shark open. Sizes: 3 small, 15 medium, 8 large.

**Flags:**
- **Six ranged carries is the new pile.** Keep his picks, but give each a different kind of carry so no two play
  alike: the falcon by range, the squirrel by moving, the hornet by on-hit stacks (good against tanks), the bombardier
  beetle by area combos (mix), the scorpion by corrosion that cuts healing (the roster's first anti-heal, which
  matters now that the jellyfish makes two healers), and the dragonfly as a strafing flier that shoots while moving in
  any direction. The scorpion and dragonfly parts are my proposals.
- **The shark's role: a water battlemage.** Changed the same day: Preston wants more mages. Its currents drag enemies
  along a flow and speed allies who go with it, from close range, which is a battlemage's job.
- **The mantis's blade forearms are magnetic** on its sheet, which is the pangolin's power. A flag for its kit.
- **The eel's electricity** sits near the falcon's storm/ion. Keep it on the ground and in another colour.
- **The pressure trio:** the hippo, the shark and the pistol shrimp. Their verbs and colours keep them apart.

**Still missing after the pipeline:**
- mages (Preston: "definitely feel like were lacking mages"): 4 of 26 (5 with the shark as a battlemage),
  and no ice, wind, nature or shadow power anywhere;
- a second assassin, and an invisible damage dealer (invisibility is only on the chameleon, a support, and on the
  cobra inside walls);
- anyone built to take towers;
- summons or deployables;
- a counter to the five catchers;
- small machines (3 of 26);
- a versatile machine that switches jobs.

**Sheet rules already learned** (`power-body-recreations.md`):
- Weapons and nozzles face forward. Preston questioned the rear guns on the beetle and the hornet.
- A level camera and the same body pitch in every view. The dragonfly's first angles made a wrong 3D model.
- Wings edge-on in the profile views.
- The right leg count. The spider was redrawn with eight.

- Check each colour against the existing machines before the sheets.

**Watch before any of these gets a kit:**
- **The owl's glide** beats all five reveals. Keep it short, and it must show when it strikes.
- **The honey badger** shortens catches; it does not ignore them, or the catchers stop working.
- **The beaver's dam** is the only wall maker (the gorilla's hex wall was not taken). It needs a rule with the cobra:
  whether a dam counts as an inner wall it can cross.
- **The lyrebird's copy** must stay weaker than the original, and some ultimates may need to be off its list.
- **The octopus's ink** sits near the chameleon's blind, which is why it is optional.
- **Two fires:** keep the firehawk's flame white-gold and short-lived, and the bison's magma orange. Fires that hurt
  join the hippo's puddles and the bison's slag.
- **Two control mages:** the wood frog controls with ice (slow, then freeze) and the spider with thread. Keep the
  spider's kit off freezes.
- **Roots:** the sloth's vines would be the fourth (the lobster's pinch, the shoebill's moon pool, the pistol
  shrimp's Hammer Down). The honey badger is the answer to all of them.
- **The anglerfish is hard to see, not invisible.** Reveals show it. The owl and the chameleon keep invisibility.
- **Three healers** (hummingbird, jellyfish, axolotl) make the scorpion's anti-heal matter more.

## 5. Suggested order

1. **Pistol shrimp:** kit approved 2026-11-16, with stuns in place of the silence (`moba-kits.md`). Next: the model
   review of its sheets.
2. **The pipeline's open kits,** one at a time, each through the four-layer check. The shark first, as the water
   battlemage, then the jellyfish (a healer) and the scorpion (the anti-heal).
3. **New sheets, mages first** (his ask): the wood frog, firehawk, albatross, sloth and anglerfish. Then the axolotl
   and honey badger (he likes both) and the owl (his invisibility ask). Then the beaver, bear, lyrebird and ant. The
   octopus stays optional.

**If every creature here is made:** 39 machines. 4 tanks, 5 fighters, 2 assassins, 2 junglers, 11 mages, 6 ranged
carries, 7 supports, 2 specialists. Sizes: 8 small, 22 medium, 9 large. Tanks become the thin role (4 of 39); the
bear, a fighter-tank, helps.
