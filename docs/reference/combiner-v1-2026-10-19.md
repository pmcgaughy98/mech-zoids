## v1 as built
`tools/blender/build_combiner_v1.py` takes the six finished machines from the factory (`docs/art/3d/<id>.blend`, the
gorilla's `ape_l_v2.blend`), freezes each in its rest pose, turns and scales it into one body part, and joins them into
`docs/art/3d/combiner_v1.blend` (9.9 m tall, 8.3 m across, 262k triangles). The factory's renderer draws its eight
views (`SPRITE_TARGET_Z=4.95 SPRITE_ORTHO=12`, a new optional setting; the default is unchanged for the six), and
`tools/art/combiner_pixelize.py` takes them down at the same x4.4 as every machine (sprites about 210 to 275 px wide,
about 2.5 times one machine). Sheet: `docs/art/2d/combiner_v1/combiner_v1_views.png`.

## What v1 shows, and what v2 needs
- The front view reads as a giant: crest, broad shoulders, two arms, two legs. The idea works as a silhouette.
- **The machines are rigid, not folded.** Their own legs and arms stick out (the gorilla's limbs make a wide slab of
  the torso; the hippo's legs point backward off the leg). v2 needs a "combined mode" pose per machine: limbs folded
  flat, tail tucked, the way each Combaticon folds into its limb. Each builder gets one more clip (like `battle`) and
  the combiner freezes that clip instead of the rest pose.
- **Joins:** the arms float a little off the torso and the two legs differ in width (hippo 1.9 m, pangolin 1.3 m).
  v2: shoulder and hip connector pieces (a gold ring like the shoebill's hips), and leg widths matched.
- **Colour:** six palettes do not merge yet (the wolf, kangaroo and pangolin reds overlap in hue, two blues, and the
  gorilla's green lives in the pixelizer's code), so v1 is quantized to 48 colours instead of the detail pixelizer.
  v2: a merged palette file so the detail pixelizer can run.
- **The game side:** combining is a mechanic Preston has not designed yet (`deploy-and-combine-scan-2026-10-19.md`).
  Nothing is wired into battles.
