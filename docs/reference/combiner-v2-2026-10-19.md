# The combination machine: synthesis and v1 model (Claude, 2026-10-19)

Preston, in order:
1. "Rather than megazord or humanoid mecha combination I'd like all the zoids to combine into one ultimate
   mecha-beast. So all parts detach and combine into one ... Expand your horizons too, transformers earth wars ...
   zoids, doesn't have to be just from mainstream ideas either."
2. "We can do it like zoids legacy, create a whole new model using mass manufacturing, the zoids will kind of get
   dragged towards the center and the circle of light that kind of looks like pokemon evolving and then out comes a
   whole new creature with a different color etc. but you can tell it has aspects of all creatures or is a
   combination of them exampled by legacy and fuzors. So you don't actually have to use the other models physically."

Nothing here is canon: the look, colours, name and the mechanic's rules are Preston's.

## What was read (R) and what is general knowledge (G)
- **Zoids Legacy, the decompilation (R, structure only):** `ZoidsLegacyDecomp/src/battle/combinations/`. Eight
  combination recipes (Two Arm Lizard, Fuzor Dragon, Chimera Dragon, Gojulox, Killer Spiner, Gojulas Giga Cannon,
  Griffin, Lord Gale). Each recipe is a formation rule plus up to six component models. Five formation rules: units
  in consecutive slots, a front and rear pair, a two-by-two block, a host plus rear units, and **all six components**.
  In battle: the components' departure animation, a camera transition, `set_battle_unit_combined_model` swaps the
  owner's model for the combined one, the components are absorbed, a popup plays; the components' original party
  slots are kept so the combination can be undone (`reset_battle_combination_presentation`).
  **The key point for us: Legacy does not glue the component models together; the combined unit is its own model.**
- **Zoids Fuzors and Blox (R, search excerpts):** "Fuzor Zoids are combinations of two or more individual Zoids or
  Blox units ... able to share power supplies" (Zoids Wiki); Blox were "meant to combine in different combinations"
  using shared cores. Liger Zero Phoenix: one beast wearing another's wings and armour (G).
- **Transformers: Earth Wars combiners (R, excerpts):** Volcanicus (the Dinobots) and Predaking; "each Dinobot
  combines as arms and legs around the" torso. That is the humanoid pattern Preston does not want; kept as the
  counter-example. Fall of Cybertron's Bruticus was measured earlier (`combiner-v1-2026-10-19.md`).
- **Non-mainstream (R, excerpts):** the Nue of Japanese folklore, "the head of a monkey, the body of a tanuki, the
  tail of a snake, and the limbs of a tiger" (Wikipedia, Yokai.com); the Greek Chimera, a lion's forebody with a goat
  head rising from the back and a snake for a tail (G); Okami's Chimera boss is drawn from the Nue (R, excerpt).

## The design rule taken from them
One new beast, its own body plan and colour, where **each component lends one readable trait** (the Nue's rule:
this one's head, that one's limbs, another's tail), not one whole body part each. v1 traits:

| From | Trait in the combined beast |
|-|-|
| gorilla | the high shoulder hump; huge forearms and gold knuckle fists as the forelegs |
| wolf | the wedge head, fangs, tall ears |
| kangaroo | big digitigrade hind legs with long feet; the thick tail root; the Pouch Cannon port in the chest |
| shoebill | the gold bill crest over the snout; folded feather wings; the dorsal head-gun |
| hippo | the barrel body; tusks at the jaw corners |
| pangolin | overlapping scale rows down the back and tail; the crimson tail cone |

Colour, its own: pearl armour, gold trim, gunmetal frame, crimson accents, teal eyes, and **six core gems** on the
chest in the six starters' colours (green, red, orange, blue, purple, gold), the one place all six show directly.

## v1 as built
- `tools/blender/build_combiner_m.py`, a factory machine like the six (the gorilla's kit, `quad_rig`, the battle
  stance): 7.5 m long, 3.1 m tall, about 1.5 times a starter; 18k triangles. Rigged; walk, idle and stance clips; no
  battle attacks yet.
- Palette `tools/art/palettes/combiner_m.json` (pearl at the top of a long grey ramp, grey gamma 0.5 so the pearl
  armour reads light; gold, crimson, and short gem ramps).
- Sprites: `docs/art/2d/combiner_m/v1c/` (eight views, through `creature_round.sh` at ortho 8.6, `pixelize_render`);
  sheet `docs/art/2d/combiner_m/combiner_m_v1_views.png`.
- Not installed in the game; working id `combiner_m` until Preston names it.
- Superseded experiments, kept for the record: `build_combiner_v1.py` (the six as humanoid limbs, Bruticus
  proportions) and `build_combiner_v2.py` (the six cut apart by bone and reassembled).

## The combination sequence (proposal, not built)
Legacy's order with Preston's description: the components slide toward the centre of their side of the field and
shrink; a ring of light closes over them (the evolution feel: the silhouettes flash white and pulse faster); a white
flash; the ring opens on the combined beast, which lands with dust and a screen shake; a name popup. In code: a new
`combine` step in `battle_logic.gd` and a `combine()` effect in `scripts/battle/vfx.gd` (the departure/arrival pair),
and the rule for when it is allowed (Legacy: a formation; ours could be all six in the party, or a meter).
