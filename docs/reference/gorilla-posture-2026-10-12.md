# Real gorilla posture, for drawing and building the gorilla machine (Claude, 2026-10-12)

Preston: the front and back reference views are AI art and get positioning wrong (knuckles on the ground, hips).
Posture comes from the real animal; the AI views guide the look only.

## Sources (read through Firecrawl, 2026-10-12)
- Wikipedia, "Knuckle-walking" (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knuckle-walking).
- "Why Do Knuckle-Walking African Apes Knuckle-Walk?", The Anatomical Record, 2018 (doi 10.1002/ar.23743).
- Knuckle-walking hand-posture study figures (ResearchGate 322576909); Britannica and teaching notes on ape pelves.
- Already used in the build: gorilla gait timing, Ito et al. 2025, Scientific Reports.

## What they say
- **Hand:** weight on the backs of the middle finger bones (middle phalanges); fingertips curled under; the outer
  fingers held clear of the ground. Mountain gorillas sometimes fist-walk or palm-walk instead.
- **Wrist and forearm: a column.** Gorillas knuckle-walk "columnar": hand and wrist in a straight, neutral line,
  the palm in line with the forearm and perpendicular to the ground (it faces backward). Wrist locked by flexed
  finger joints and extended knuckle joints.
- **Elbow:** extended (straight) while the hand carries weight.
- **Hind limbs:** propulsion by extending hip, knee and ankle (so they are bent at rest and in stance). Foot details
  not in these sources.
- **Pelvis:** long and narrow, with a tall, narrow ilium; the hips sit at the back end of a long trunk.
- Not stated by these sources, still to verify: back slope angle, arm-to-leg length ratio, hind foot posture.

## For the sprite
Front view: the fists read as a bar of knuckles on the ground under straight, columnar forearms, not fingers
reaching forward. Rear view: a visible pelvis (hip blades, a sacrum at the base of the spine) with the thighs
coming out of it, not legs hanging from a flat belly.
