Preston's direction: one boss gives a gold boost; the other grants a buff allowing siege or winning team fights. Study League's Herald and drakes and SMITE's Gold Fury and Fire Giant. No implementation requested. Names, animals, rewards and numbers below remain proposals.

## Structural lessons and proposal

Gold Fury: economy reward can improve the team's next purchase. Herald: a concrete siege asset converts a neutral objective into pressure. Elemental drakes/Soul: cumulative permanent rewards make future spawns increasingly important. Baron: a temporary siege window still requires players to escort a wave. Fire Giant: temporary personal power and sustain can support both fights and siege. Elder: execution is a much harder combat deadline than a modest power buff. These gameplay implications are our interpretation of the reward structures.

Proposed gold boss: immediate gold for every teammate, including teammates away from the pit or dead. Keep it distinct from permanent income stacking; Preston has not yet specified whether "gold boost" means a payout or income multiplier. Make this the earlier, quicker objective, with a visible sweep or frontal attack that exposes the team to a contest. Value should be judged against a meaningful purchase once the economy is settled.

Proposed power boss: later, tougher objective; temporary team buff with modest combat power, additional structure damage, and repair between fights. Death removes the individual's buff, proposed. Defenders can clear waves, pick off a holder or delay the push until expiry. Avoid starting with a permanent stacking advantage or an execute mechanic. Exact duration, recipients and strengths await Preston.

Keep the existing pits and beacon in the decision: pits already strengthen waves and the beacon disables an enemy tower. Do not stack all three siege rewards into an automatic base loss. Candidate division: pits improve minions, beacon grants a brief opening, boss empowers the mechs. Assess their combined effect before tuning boss reward numbers.

First eventual implementation slice: one boss and its reward in the real match, with contest, securing team, death/reset and reward delivery checked. No code, art, audio or balance changed in this research pass. Boss identities and exact encounter moves remain open.
